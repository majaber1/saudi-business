# Saudi Business brand guidelines

## Brand decision

- English name: **Saudi Business**
- Primary Arabic customer name: **سعودي بزنس**
- Descriptive Arabic lockup: **سعودي بزنس — من الفكرة إلى المشروع**

`أعمال السعودية` is formal but sounds institutional and can be confused with a
publisher or government directory. `سعودي بزنس` is more memorable and already
has product equity; the descriptive slogan supplies clarity without renaming the
repository or company.

## Positioning

Saudi Business is a bilingual digital business advisor that helps an ordinary
entrepreneur move from an idea to a clearer, evidence-aware business decision.
It is decision support—not a bank, regulator, financial guarantee or substitute
for professional legal/tax advice.

Primary slogan:

- Arabic: **من الفكرة إلى المشروع**
- English: **From Idea to Business**

Secondary slogan:

- Arabic: **خطوتك أوضح، وقرارك أفضل**
- English: **Clearer steps. Better business decisions.**

Other evaluated candidates:

| Arabic | English | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| فكرتك تبدأ بخطوة واضحة | Give your idea a clear first step | Friendly and action-led; useful campaign line. |
| اعرف قبل أن تبدأ | Know before you start | Memorable, but too narrow for growth-stage users. |
| مشروعك، خطوة بخطوة | Your business, step by step | Clear journey language, less distinctive. |
| قرارات أذكى لمشروع أقوى | Smarter decisions for a stronger business | Strong value line, slightly generic. |

Elevator pitch: **سعودي بزنس يرشد رواد الأعمال في السعودية من اختيار الفكرة ودراسة جدواها إلى فهم التمويل والفرص وتحديد الخطوة التالية بلغة واضحة.**

30-word English description: Saudi Business guides Saudi entrepreneurs from
idea discovery and feasibility to funding, opportunities, readiness and launch
planning through clear bilingual tools, transparent assumptions and practical
next actions.

## Voice

- Clear before clever; explain what, why and next action.
- Professional Saudi Modern Arabic, friendly but not slang-heavy.
- Never imply guaranteed returns, official endorsement or verified live data
  unless provenance supports the statement.
- Put technical finance terms under **تفاصيل متقدمة / Advanced details**.

## Typography

- Arabic: Tajawal, with system Arabic fallbacks.
- English: Inter, with system sans-serif fallbacks.
- Minimum body size 16px for primary journeys; 14px only for supporting metadata.
- Maintain visible focus rings and WCAG AA contrast.

## Logo architecture

Concept: a continuous **S-shaped path** formed by two rising steps and an open
destination point. It represents Saudi Business, progress, a guided route and
opportunity without using a literal Saudi map or government-style emblem.

Required asset family:

- `logo-horizontal.svg`: icon plus bilingual/locale-specific wordmark.
- `logo-icon.svg`: square path mark for app/favicon.
- `logo-light.svg`: white wordmark/mark for emerald backgrounds.
- `logo-dark.svg`: ink/emerald version for light backgrounds.
- `favicon.svg`: simplified mark legible at 16px.

SVG geometry must be original, use currentColor where practical and preserve a
clear-space area equal to one quarter of the icon width.
